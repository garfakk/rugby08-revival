A brand new Rugby 08 experience with updated teams, players, kits and new functionalities!  


This mod was inspired by the incredible work of [The Rugby Forum](https://www.therugbyforum.com/forums/ea-rugby-08.92) community over nearly 20 years.  
A huge thanks to them!
